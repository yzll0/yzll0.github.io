I am Weixuan Yuan (袁伟轩), a Ph.D. student in Computer Science at ETH Zürich, advised by Prof. Rasmus Kyng. My main research area is graph algorithms. I also work on AI for theoretical computer science and mathematics, with interests in AI-assisted discovery, problem solving, and formal verification, as well as LLM reasoning and post-training.

I received my M.Sc. in Data Science from ETH Zürich in 2024 and my B.Sc. in Mathematics (Honors Youth Program) from Xi'an Jiaotong University in 2022.

## Selected research

**Graph algorithms.** Our [FOCS 2025 paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.20354) gives the first deterministic almost-linear-time algorithm for constructing Gomory-Hu trees. I presented the paper with Wuwei Yuan at the conference. [[slides](https://ywwywwyww.github.io/files/DeterGHTreeSlides.pdf)]

**AuXSpec.** We study how to train language models to translate natural-language programming problems into formal specifications in Lean. Our approach uses executable postconditions to test generated specifications efficiently and provide feedback for model training. Co-first author; under review at ICLR 2027.

See my [selected publications](/publications/) for paper details and my [CV](/cv/) ([PDF](/files/Weixuan_Yuan_CV.pdf)) for research experience.
