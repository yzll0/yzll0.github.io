I am Weixuan Yuan (袁伟轩), a Ph.D. student in Computer Science at ETH Zürich, advised by Prof. Rasmus Kyng. My main research area is graph algorithms. I also work on AI for theoretical computer science and mathematics, with interests in AI-assisted discovery, problem solving, and formal verification, as well as LLM reasoning and post-training.

I received my M.Sc. in Data Science from ETH Zürich in 2024 and my B.Sc. in Mathematics from Xi'an Jiaotong University in 2022. I received the [2025 Willi Studer Prize](https://inf.ethz.ch/news-and-events/spotlights/infk-news-channel/2025/09/eth-medals-and-willi-studer-prizes-for-masters-graduates.html) as the best graduate of ETH's Master's programme in Data Science.

## Selected research

**Graph algorithms.** Our [FOCS 2025 paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.20354) gives the first deterministic almost-linear-time algorithm for Gomory-Hu trees. I presented the paper at the conference. [[slides](https://ywwywwyww.github.io/files/DeterGHTreeSlides.pdf)]

**AuXSpec.** We study how executable postconditions can support the generation and verification of Lean program specifications. Co-first author; under review at ICLR 2027.

See my [selected publications](/publications/) for paper details and my [CV](/cv/) ([PDF](/files/Weixuan_Yuan_CV.pdf)) for research experience.
