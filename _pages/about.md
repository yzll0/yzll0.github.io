I am Weixuan Yuan (袁伟轩), a Ph.D. student in Computer Science at ETH Zürich, advised by Prof. Rasmus Kyng. My research spans graph algorithms and AI for mathematics and theoretical computer science. On the AI side, I work on LLM reasoning and post-training with verifiable feedback, formal verification in Lean, and AI-assisted discovery.

I received my M.Sc. in Data Science from ETH Zürich in 2024 and my B.Sc. in Mathematics (Honors Youth Program) from Xi'an Jiaotong University in 2022.

**I am looking for research internships starting January 2027 (3–6 months, location flexible).** See my [CV](/cv/) ([PDF](/files/Weixuan_Yuan_CV.pdf)).

## Selected research

**AuXSpec.** We study how to train language models to translate natural-language programming problems into formal specifications in Lean. Our approach pairs each specification with an executable postcondition that can be checked on any test case, giving a cheap, automatic signal for verifying generated specifications and for SFT/RL training. Co-first author; under review at ICLR 2027.

**Graph algorithms.** Our [FOCS 2025 paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.20354) gives the first deterministic almost-linear-time algorithm for constructing Gomory-Hu trees (all-pairs minimum cuts). I presented the paper with Wuwei Yuan at the conference. [[slides](https://ywwywwyww.github.io/files/DeterGHTreeSlides.pdf)]
