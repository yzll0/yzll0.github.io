I am Weixuan Yuan (袁伟轩), a Ph.D. student in Computer Science at ETH Zürich, advised by Prof. Rasmus Kyng. I started my Ph.D. in May 2025, after working as a research assistant in the same group from October 2024 to April 2025.

My research interests include theoretical computer science, graph algorithms, and AI for mathematics and theoretical computer science. I study fast graph algorithms and how AI can help solve, formalize, and verify theoretical problems. I am also interested in LLM reasoning and post-training.

I received my M.Sc. in Data Science from ETH Zürich in October 2024 and my B.Sc. in Mathematics from Xi'an Jiaotong University in July 2022. I received the [2025 Willi Studer Prize](https://inf.ethz.ch/news-and-events/spotlights/infk-news-channel/2025/09/eth-medals-and-willi-studer-prizes-for-masters-graduates.html) as the best graduate of ETH's Master's programme in Data Science.

## Selected research

**Graph algorithms.** Our [FOCS 2025 paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.20354) gives the first deterministic almost-linear-time algorithm for constructing Gomory-Hu trees, running in <i>m</i><sup>1+o(1)</sup> time. I presented the paper at FOCS 2025. [[slides](https://ywwywwyww.github.io/files/DeterGHTreeSlides.pdf)]

**AI and formal verification.** In *AuXSpec: Learning to Autoformalize Program Specifications via Executable Postconditions*, I proposed the executable-postcondition approach and co-developed a multistage data synthesis pipeline for Lean specifications. Co-first author; under review at ICLR 2027.
